Key Takeaway

My approach to digital design connects user needs, business objectives, and technical feasibility throughout the product lifecycle. I use design systems to establish consistency, high-fidelity screen designs to translate requirements into interfaces, and prototypes to support shared decisions before implementation. Through ongoing collaboration with product, engineering, and stakeholders, I help digital experiences evolve without losing visual coherence, accessibility, or alignment with established design standards.

I connect design intent with implementation reality.
