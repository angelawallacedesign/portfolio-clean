Nickelodeon interactive tv

Case Study

Overview

As part of a UX/UI team, I designed the television interface for Nickelodeon Hotels & Resorts Punta Cana guest suites. The work brought Nickelodeon’s visual identity into an in-room experience guests could read from a distance and navigate with a remote.

Design priorities

  • Keep text and controls legible across the room
  • Make remote-control navigation simple and predictable
  • Carry the resort’s brand across guest touchpoints

Researching the brand

I reviewed Nickelodeon’s style guidance and public-facing materials, including photography, copy, and visual details. I synthesized brand consistencies along with interior design, graphic design and architecture related to the client. Those references helped the TV screens feel connected to the wider resort experience while meeting the practical needs of the room.

I made note of copy voice and tone, photography style, demographics, target audiences, and considerations such as where the app would be used, visibility or accessibility factors, and existing client business units.

  • Compared brand references and existing guest touchpoints
  • Considered audience, setting, and visibility
  • Applied the approved direction to the existing app framework

Design Walkthrough

I started with the home screen and selected inner screens so the client could review the branded experience before the remaining screens were produced. Feedback was discussed with design and development, then carried into the next round of mockups.

Native Icon Sets

I was one of the senior designers at the beginning, and toward the end became the Lead UI Designer of the project. Using annotated wireframes and mockups as a guide, I created and exported native graphic assets following the required file and folder naming conventions. Graphics followed Human Interface Guidelines for Apple devices and Material Design guidelines for Android devices. I created interactive TV graphics in SVG format where they made sense and styled the interface front end using HTML and CSS.
