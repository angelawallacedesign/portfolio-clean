Social profile integration

Allin Interactive · Mobile UI/UX Case Study

Designing social profile connections and guest discovery within an established, multi-brand hospitality mobile app.

Overview

DigiMobile was a guest-facing hospitality mobile platform adapted for different cruise and resort brands. My work included applying client brand guidelines to an existing application framework and designing a social profile integration that worked alongside the established communication features.

Project Summary

Company
Allin Interactive
Role
Lead UX Designer
Platform
DigiMobile · hospitality mobile app
Focus
Social account integration, profiles, guest discovery
Collaboration
Engineering, Design, QA, Cruise Implementation

Challenge

Extend a shared mobile product with optional social account connections, profile editing and guest discovery while keeping the experience understandable within the existing communication menu.

Social opt-in & onboarding

The documented flow begins with cabin registration or linking and profile selection or creation. Guests could opt into social features, choose a social network, authenticate, grant permissions, or continue without connecting an account.

Profile & photo customization

The wireframes cover optional About Me, age, gender, relationship status and interests fields. Profile photos could come from the camera, an upload or a connected social account, followed by cropping, repositioning and saving.

Guest discovery & privacy

Guests could search by name, age range, gender, interests and relationship status, inspect a guest profile and add or block contacts. Privacy settings included hiding from search, restricting calls and chats to contacts, and Do Not Disturb.

Existing communication features

The social experience appeared alongside the existing phone, chat, mail and social board options. Chat was already designed; the case study focuses on the social integration and related profile and discovery flows.

Presentation & reconstruction

Original wireframes and flow diagrams survive, but the final social-feature screens are not available. New high-fidelity screens should be labeled as reconstructions informed by the original wireframes and branded DigiMobile interface references, not screenshots of the shipped feature.
